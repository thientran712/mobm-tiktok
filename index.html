Museum of Bad Money is a video channel about the history of scams, frauds and financial disasters — told as museum exhibits, with real evidence.

This site hosts the policies for our internal publishing tool, which uploads our own finished videos to our own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API. The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's account.
